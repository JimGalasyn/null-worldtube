The framework's \emph{discrete} structural commitments---the closed form $\alpha = 1/(25\pi\sqrt{3} + 1)$, the algebra $\so(7) = B_3$, the disk curvature $\alpha_{\mathrm{KL}} = 16/25$, and the prefactor integers ($3/14$, $8/7$, $18$, $25$)---are fixed by the substrate construction rather than fitted to data, but are theoretical choices, not consequences of a deeper dynamical principle established here. The lone dimensional input, $\Mpl$, \emph{cancels from every dimensionless prediction} (verified identically at $\Mpl$ and at $10^6\,\Mpl$). We give the inheritance mechanism, the derived observable ledger, and the sharp falsifiers---$r = 48\alpha^2 = 2.56\times10^{-3}$ ($\sim\!5\sigma$ detection by CMB-S4 at design sensitivity, $2$--$3\sigma$ discrimination from the Starobinsky benchmark) and a $98$~GeV dark-matter state (LZ-G3/DARWIN)---and we demarcate honestly the one quantity that is fixed but not derived (the inflaton-disk radius) and the one that is astrophysical (the event rate). This is the cosmogenic companion to Papers 21a/21b~\cite{Paper21a,Paper21b}, supplying the $\Mpl$-fixing mechanism those papers forward-reference.
